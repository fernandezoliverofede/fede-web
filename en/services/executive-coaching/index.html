Executive Coaching & Advisory

A confidential space for the thinking that doesn't happen in the boardroom — to challenge assumptions, clarify what matters, and make better decisions about what kind of leader you want to be.

The thinking that rarely gets space

Senior leaders and executives face a particular challenge: the more senior the role, the fewer people in the organization can offer genuinely candid perspective. Peers have their own interests. Direct reports are careful. Board members have limited context. And the pressure to appear confident and decisive leaves little room for the exploratory thinking that good leadership requires.

Executive coaching creates the space for that thinking — a confidential, structured partnership with someone whose only interest is helping you lead more effectively.

What this work covers

  • Navigating complex organizational decisions — restructuring, strategy, people
  • Leadership transitions — new role, expanded scope, different organizational context
  • Working through interpersonal dynamics at the top — board relationships, senior team tensions
  • Developing a clearer sense of your own leadership identity and how you want to lead
  • Thinking through significant career decisions with appropriate depth
  • Building the reflective capacity to lead more intentionally

Coaching with an organizational lens

My coaching is grounded in an organizational development perspective. That means I'm not just interested in you as an individual — I'm interested in you as a leader operating within a specific organizational context.

Often the most useful coaching conversations aren't just about what you're doing or how you're doing it, but about the organizational dynamics shaping the situation you're navigating. That broader perspective is something I bring to every coaching engagement.

Coaching engagements are typically structured around a series of conversations over several months, with a cadence and format tailored to your context and what you're working through.

Who this is for

CEOs, founders, and C-suite executives navigating significant decisions or transitions. Senior leaders who want a confidential space to think, develop, and lead with greater intentionality. Leaders who value candor and are willing to examine their own assumptions and behaviors.
