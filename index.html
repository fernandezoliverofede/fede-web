A point of view

Most organizational challenges aren't talent problems.

People don't underperform in isolation. They underperform inside structures that make good work difficult — where decision rights are unclear, accountability is uneven, and leadership behaviors don't match what strategy requires.

My work starts from that premise. Understanding the system before prescribing anything. Fixing the conditions, not just developing the people inside them.

"Most companies don't lack ambitious strategies.
They lack organizations capable of executing them."
That gap is where I work.

I've spent 15+ years at the intersection of business performance and people development — in corporate HR, as an external consultant, as an executive coach, and as a university instructor. What I've found consistently: strategy fails in the operating model, not in the boardroom.

A company grows faster than its decision-making structure can support.
A capable leadership team struggles to align around priorities.
A culture that worked at 50 people becomes harder to sustain at 300.
A strategy is clear, but execution breaks down between teams.
Expertise

Four areas. One objective.

These aren't four separate services. They're four ways of addressing the same underlying challenge: building organizations that can do what they intend to do.

01

Organizational Development

Helping companies improve how responsibilities are defined, decisions are made, and teams collaborate — so that structure supports strategy rather than working against it.

Clarifying roles and decision rights. Redesigning how teams collaborate. Identifying where the operating model creates friction. Building organizational capability to sustain change after the engagement ends.
CEOs and founders managing rapid growth or post-merger integration. Organizations where strategy keeps stalling at the execution level. HR leaders who need a credible external perspective on structural issues.
02

Executive Leadership

Strengthening the leadership capabilities that drive organizational performance — not through generic programs, but through targeted work on the specific behaviors that matter for this business, now.

Custom leadership development programs. Leadership assessments and 360 feedback processes. Experiential workshops designed around real business challenges. Longer journeys (3–6 months) for sustained behavior change.
Companies that need their leadership layer to grow alongside the business. Organizations investing in the development of their next generation of executives. HR leaders building a leadership development architecture from scratch.
03

Culture & Change

Culture is what people actually do, not what's written on the walls. Helping organizations understand the gap between declared values and daily behaviors — and close it deliberately.

Culture diagnostics and organizational listening. Change management design and implementation support. Building leadership behaviors that reinforce the culture the business needs. Helping leaders understand that culture change starts with them.
Companies navigating transformations where culture is a known risk. Organizations where performance has plateaued without an obvious operational reason. Leaders who sense that something about "how we work" needs to change but can't name exactly what.
04

Executive Coaching & Advisory

A thinking partnership for executives navigating complex decisions, transitions, and leadership challenges — the kind that don't fit neatly into a meeting agenda.

One-to-one coaching for C-suite leaders and high-potential executives. Support during role transitions and new mandates. Real-time advisory during strategic inflection points. Shadow coaching and in-context support for leaders who want to work on live challenges.
Leaders who are effective but want to be more deliberate about how they lead. Executives facing decisions that involve uncertainty, competing priorities, or high personal stakes. Organizations investing in the development of specific key individuals.
How I work

Diagnosis before prescription.

Every engagement begins with understanding the actual situation — not the presenting problem, but the system behind it. What's really constraining performance here?

01
Diagnose Understand the system — what the organization needs, where the real constraints are, and what interventions will actually move the needle.
02
Align Build shared clarity at the leadership level on priorities, expectations, and the definition of success. Misalignment at the top makes every intervention less effective.
03
Activate Design and deliver targeted interventions — coaching, programs, structural changes — specific to the organization and the moment.
04
Sustain Embed the change. Build internal capability so performance doesn't depend on the consultant staying in the room.
